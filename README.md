- A Java simulation was developed for an art sales system that manages the information of various works, artists and clients interested in acquiring these works.

- The system incorporates a main menu that facilitates a range of operations, including the display of a list of available works, the execution of searches based on title, artist or year of creation, and the insertion, modification or deletion of works. Additionally, the system incorporates functionalities for the management of client information, encompassing search, addition, modification, and deletion of client records, in addition to facilitating the acquisition of artistic creations.

- The software is further enhanced by the provision of sample text files containing data on existing works, artists and clients, which can be used to efficiently upload and manage information in the system.
